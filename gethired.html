Questions

Good to know.

Is GetHired free to use?

+

Yes. There's no cost, no paywall, and no login required to screen a CV or take the practical assessment.

Does GetHired use AI to grade my CV or assessment?

+

No. GetHired is deliberately rule-based end to end — weighted keyword matching for the CV screener, exact-match checkpoint grading for the practical assessment, and config-driven logic for the roadmap. No LLM or external AI API is called anywhere in the product, by design — partly for cost reasons, partly so every score is explainable and reproducible.

What if I don't have hands-on experience yet?

+

That's exactly who GetHired is built for. The practical assessment doesn't require any lab setup or prior tooling — it's a set of static, self-contained scenarios you can attempt with no infrastructure of your own. The roadmap it produces is meant to be the starting point, not a gate you have to already pass.

Does a non-CS or non-traditional background hurt my score?

+

No. Education is shown as informational context only — it's explicitly excluded from the overall score and never generates a "get a different degree" recommendation. What's scored is skills, tools, and demonstrated concepts.
